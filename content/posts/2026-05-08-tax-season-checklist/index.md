## Collect the right information early

Make tax-form collection part of onboarding and the payment workflow. Keep the creator’s legal payee details with the relevant W-9 or W-8, rather than looking for them after a campaign has finished.

## Check the reporting rules for the payment year

For payments made after December 31, 2025, the general federal Form 1099-NEC reporting threshold is $2,000. Exceptions and other reporting obligations can apply. Review the current IRS guidance with your accountant for your agency’s circumstances.

## Reconcile regularly

Match your campaign agreements, invoices, and completed payouts during the year. Confirm which entity received each payment and keep the records together. A recurring review makes questions easier to resolve while the campaign is still familiar.
