## Agree the split with the campaign

A campaign can have several people behind it. Set their percentages with the deal, rather than calculating them again every time a payment arrives. The agreed allocation becomes the reference for the payment workflow.

## One payment, several shares

For a $20,000 brand payment, a 65% creator share is $13,000, a 20% agency share is $4,000, and a 15% manager share is $3,000. Riff calculates those amounts from the approved percentages.

## Keep the payment with the work

Approve the splits upfront, collect the required tax forms, and keep the invoice linked to the campaign. Creator payouts run automatically once brand funds clear, so the team has one place to follow the payment.
