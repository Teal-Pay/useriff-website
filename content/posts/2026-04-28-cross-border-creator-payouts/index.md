## Keep the campaign in one place

International campaigns still have the same essentials: the agreed fee, the recipient, the currency, and the payment terms. Keeping these details with the deal gives your team a reliable starting point.

## Look beyond the transfer amount

When reviewing a payment route, consider the processing fee, currency conversion, recipient fees, and expected settlement time together. The amount a creator receives matters as much as the amount the agency sends.

## Give the team a shared payment record

Riff supports multi-currency splits and global creator payouts within the campaign workflow. Keep the invoice, allocation, and payout progress together, so a cross-border campaign does not require a separate tracker.
